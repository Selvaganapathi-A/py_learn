# 🚫 Just Because You Can, Doesn’t Mean You Should

## Principle:

**Capability ≠ Justification.**

---

## In Practice:

* ✅ You *can* write raw SQL in every route
  🔥 You *should* use an ORM for clarity and safety.

* ✅ You *can* deploy to prod on a Friday
  🔥 You *shouldn’t* unless you love weekend war rooms.

* ✅ You *can* automate decisions with AI
  🔥 You *shouldn’t* when human context matters.

* ✅ You *can* over-engineer a feature
  🔥 You *should* solve the problem, not build a monument.

---

## Why It Matters:

> **Discipline, not capability, defines great engineers and great teams.**
> We measure success not just by what we *build*, but by what we *choose not to*.
